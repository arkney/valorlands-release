Valorlands Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Valorlands is an online player-versus-player game made by Arkney Interactive. This page explains what we collect when you play, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: arkneyinteractive@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your username, and if you create an email account, your email address and a hashed password. Guest accounts use an identifier generated for your device. If you link Google or Apple sign-in, we store the account identifier they give us.
  • Country: worked out from your connection when you log in, and shown as a flag next to your name.
  • Sign-in and device records: each time you log in we record when you signed in and out, your IP address, the approximate location derived from it by our network provider (country, region and city, never GPS or precise location), your game version and platform, and your device model, operating system, graphics chip, screen size and language. Only the game's operator can see these records. Other players and in-game moderators cannot.
  • Activity records: what you do in the game, such as matches played, items equipped or enhanced, chests opened, trades, market activity and changes to your currencies, with the time of each.
  • Game data: level, currencies, items, match results and statistics, rankings, achievements and titles, clan membership, friends, trades and market activity. Rankings and your public profile are visible to other players.
  • Messages: world, clan and private chat, which we store so they can be delivered and so abuse can be handled.
  • Discord: if you choose to link a Discord account, we store your Discord user ID and display name and when you linked it, and we give you roles on our Discord server. On PC, if Discord is running, the game can show that you are playing on your Discord profile. We count the minutes this is shown while your account is linked, up to two hours a day, and use that count for in-game rewards. We also record which rewards you received. You can unlink at any time in Settings > Account or on Discord.
  • Feedback: what you send through the in-game feedback menu, with any contact detail you choose to add and basic context such as game version, platform, language and your class and level.
  • Player reports: if you report a player or are reported, we keep the report, the category and comment, and the chat lines that go with it, including private messages between the people involved where they are relevant to the report.
  • Mobile only: a push notification token if you allow notifications. If you agree to the analytics prompt, anonymous usage and crash data as well.

Why we use it

To run your account and matches, keep rankings fair, deliver chat and notifications, fix bugs, balance the game, secure accounts, and deal with cheating, multiple-account abuse and other misuse, for which we may compare the sign-in records of different accounts. We do not sell your personal data. Valorlands does not show ads inside the game.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare carries the connection between the game and our servers, sees your IP address to do so, and tells us your approximate location.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications on mobile, and receives analytics and crash data if you agreed to them.
  • Meta receives app events on mobile if you agreed to the analytics prompt, so we can measure our ads.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you link those accounts.
  • Discord receives what you do on its service, and, if you link your account or run Discord on PC, the fact that you are playing Valorlands. Discord's own privacy policy applies there.

These providers may process data outside your country.

Your choices

  • You can change the analytics choice in the game settings at any time.
  • You can turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • You can unlink your Discord account at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account in the game under Settings > Account, or by emailing us. Your request waits 30 days, during which you can sign in and cancel it. After that we erase your account and the data listed here: your profile, items and currencies, chat messages including private messages sent to you, sign-in and activity records, friends, clan membership, Discord link, feedback and reports. If you led a clan, it passes to another member. We keep only what is needed to prevent abuse or meet legal duties: that an account with that identifier was deleted, moderation records with your name removed, and, if you received a Discord link reward, your Discord ID, so that the reward cannot be claimed twice.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, including your sign-in and activity records, or to correct it by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or KVKK, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account exists. IP addresses and the region and city derived from them are kept for 90 days and then erased. Other sign-in and activity records are kept for 12 months. After that we keep only totals that do not identify you. Feedback is kept for as long as it is useful for improving the game. Server logs that may contain your username are kept for a short time for running the service. Encrypted backups are kept for 14 days, so data you have had erased can remain in them until they expire.

Children

Valorlands is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell players in the game.
