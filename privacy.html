Valorlands Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Valorlands is an online role-playing game for mobile, made by Arkney Interactive. This page explains what we collect when you play, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: arkneyinteractive@gmail.com.

This policy covers the current version of Valorlands (version 0.5 and later). An earlier, different game was published under the same name until November 2026. The data it held is covered by the section on the earlier game below.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password (we never store the password itself), when the account was created and when you last signed in, and which versions of these terms and this policy you accepted, and when.
  • Characters and progress: character names, faction, class, level, experience, skills, items, gold, bank, quests, professions, dungeon entries, honor and weekly rankings, event points, and your clan, friends and block list. Character names, clan names and emblems, and rankings are visible to other players.
  • Activity records: trades between players, market sales, other movements of gold and items, every upgrade attempt and its result, when each game session started and ended and on which server, and changes to your Valor Crystal balance. We keep these to settle disputes, restore lost items and find cheating.
  • Security and moderation records: automatic alerts when the server sees something unusual on a character (such as impossible movement, unusual gains or signs of a bot), commands used by game masters, and mutes, kicks and bans with their reason.
  • Chat: party, clan and private messages are passed on to the players who should receive them and are not stored. Lines in the public channels (general, zone and trade) are also kept for 30 days for the in-game assistant described below. Messages the server rejects (for example too fast or too long) are noted in the server log without their text. Game master commands typed in chat are recorded as described above.
  • Discord link: if you link your Discord account, we store your Discord user ID and display name and when you linked it, give you a role on our Discord server, and record the one-time link reward so it cannot be claimed twice.
  • Purchases: when you buy Valor Crystals on Android, Google Play handles the payment. We receive the product, the order number and a purchase token, which we check with Google and store only as a one-way fingerprint, so the same purchase cannot be credited twice. We never see your payment details. Purchases are not available on iOS at this time.
  • Error reports: if the game crashes or hits an error, it sends us the game version, platform, operating system version, device model, language, graphics mode and the last lines of the game log, linked to your account if you were signed in. We do not store your IP address with these reports.
  • Connection data: our servers see your IP address while you are connected. We use it only to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts, and do not store it with your account.

The game does not use advertising or analytics services, does not show ads, and does not collect your precise location, contacts or photos.

Why we use it

To run your account and characters, keep the game fair, deliver chat, trades and purchases, fix bugs, balance the game, keep accounts secure, and deal with cheating and other misuse. We do not sell your personal data.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare carries the connection between the game and our account service and sees your IP address to do so.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model behind the in-game and Discord assistants and receives the lines described in those sections.
  • Discord receives the role we give you if you link your account. Discord's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Google Play handles purchases on Android and lets us check them. Google's and Apple's own policies apply to their stores and to TestFlight.
  • Our servers run on a machine we rent from a hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf.

These providers may process data outside your country.

In-game assistant

An automated assistant answers questions in the game's public chat channels (general, zone and trade). It does not read party, clan or private messages. When a line looks like a question, is addressed to the assistant, or a new character enters the game, that line and the recent public lines around it are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write a reply, which appears in the chat under the name "Moderator". Character names and the text of the lines are sent, so don't post personal details in public chat. We keep the public lines, the replies and the character names for 30 days so we can check its work. It has no access to your account, it can be wrong, and a person can review its answers.

Our Discord server

Our community Discord server has an automated assistant that answers questions about the game. It reads messages in the server's public channels. When a message looks like a question, a greeting from someone new, or is addressed to the assistant, that message and the recent messages around it in the channel are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write a reply. Your Discord user ID and username are not sent with them, but anything you write in the message is, so don't post personal details. We keep the messages it answered, the replies, and your Discord user ID and username for 30 days so we can check its work. It has no access to your game account, it can be wrong, and a person can review its answers. Discord's own privacy policy applies to the service itself.

Your choices

  • Copy of your data: in the game under Settings > Account, you can ask for a copy of your account, characters and records. It is prepared as a file you can download in the game for 7 days.
  • Deleting your account: in the game under Settings > Account, with your password, or by emailing us. Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. Your email address and password are erased from the account, your Discord link is removed, your characters are removed from the game and their names become free, your sessions end, and any Valor Crystals left on the account are forfeited. If you led a clan, it passes to another member. We keep the activity, security and purchase records described above, without your email address, because they are needed to prevent abuse, settle disputes and meet legal duties.
  • Discord link: remove it in the game under Settings > Account > Discord, or with /unlink on our Discord server. The record that the reward was given stays, so it cannot be claimed twice.
  • Deleting a character: in the game on the character screen. The character is removed after a short waiting period, during which you can restore it.
  • You can ask us to correct your data or answer questions about it by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or KVKK, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account exists. Activity, security and purchase records are kept for as long as they are needed for the reasons above, and at most for the time the law requires us to keep purchase records. Error reports are kept while they help us fix the problem they describe. Prepared data copies are erased after 7 days. Server logs are kept for a short time for running the service. Copies of data in server backups are erased when those backups expire, after at most 14 days.

The earlier game

The earlier game published as Valorlands, and its Instagram assistant (stopped on November 25, 2026), kept accounts, sign-in records and messages as described in the policy that applied then. That data is not used by the current game. To have it deleted, or to get a copy, email us.

Children

Valorlands is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, ask you to accept the new version in the game.
